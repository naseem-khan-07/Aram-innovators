OUR PROBLEM STATEMENT

A deployable AI-powered autonomous drone that aids search-and-rescue operations by detecting people and hazards, thereby improving responder safety and reducing victim discovery time.

ID: 26177 Organization: Qualcomm Inc Category: Hardware Theme: Robotics & Drones

Background & Objective

India’s vast and diverse geography makes it highly vulnerable to natural disasters such as floods, landslides, and earthquakes. Traditional search and rescue operations are often hindered by inaccessible terrain, hazardous conditions, and delays in initial response. The objective is to design a highly capable, autonomous aerial platform that acts as a first responder, establishing situational awareness and rapidly pinpointing victims before ground teams arrive, significantly increasing survival rates.

System Description

Our solution is an end-to-end autonomous drone platform that seamlessly integrates a multi-modal sensor suite (RGB, Thermal, UWB Radar) with cutting-edge Edge AI processing. Operating independently of cloud infrastructure, it navigates complex, GPS-denied environments using advanced SLAM techniques. Upon identifying survivors and assessing surrounding hazards, it relays encrypted, geo-tagged intelligence directly to field commanders, ensuring a swift and coordinated rescue effort.

Our Proposed Solution

End-to-End Autonomous Disaster Response System

Our comprehensive system integrates robust hardware, advanced multi-modal AI, and secure communications to create a highly autonomous aerial rescue platform. By fusing data from multiple sensors at the edge, we achieve high-confidence detection in complex environments with zero cloud dependency.

  • Sensor Suite
  • Edge AI Pipeline
  • Navigation & SLAM
  • Communication & GCS
  • Detection Pipeline
  • Innovation & USP
  • OAK-D Lite Stereo Camera — 4 TOPS AI, 30 FPS RGB+depth, 72° FoV, USB-C
  • MLX90640 Thermal Array — 32×24 IR, −40°C to +300°C, 55° FoV, I²C at 16 Hz
  • X4M200 UWB Radar — Respiration + micro-motion, 0.4–5 m, ISM 7.29 GHz, UART
  • RPLIDAR A1 — 360° 2D scan, 12 m range, 8000 sps, UART
  • TF02-Pro LiDAR — Single-point, 40 m, 100 Hz, I²C / UART
  • H-Flow Optical Flow — PMW3901 + VL53L1X ToF, SPI
  • M10 GNSS — GPS L1 + GLONASS, 10 Hz, UART
  • Companion Computer: Radxa ROCK 5C — RK3588S2 SoC, 8 GB LPDDR4X, 6 TOPS NPU, USB 3.0, GPIO 40-pin, passive cooling
  • AI Model: YOLOv8n trained on VisDrone + disaster-specific datasets. Exported to RKNN INT8 for 6 TOPS NPU.
  • Inference Pipeline: Camera → RKNN YOLOv8n → Bounding Boxes → Multi-sensor Confirmation → Geo-tagged Alert
  • Key capabilities: Person detection, fire/smoke detection, collapsed structures, flood water, debris
  • Edge-first architecture: No cloud dependency, <200ms latency, works in connectivity-denied environments
  • Flight Controller: Pixhawk 6C Mini — STM32H743, dual IMU, barometer, MAVLink 2.0
  • Dual-Mode Navigation:
    • Mode A (Outdoor): M10 GNSS + barometric altitude → waypoint following via ArduPilot/PX4
    • Mode B (GPS-denied): VIO (OpenVINS on OAK-D stereo) + LiDAR SLAM (SLAM Toolbox with RPLIDAR A1)
  • Autonomous Path Planning: Nav2 stack (ROS 2 Humble) with TEB local planner, costmap from LiDAR, dynamic obstacle avoidance
  • Altitude Hold: TF02-Pro single-point LiDAR + barometer fusion
  • Software Stack: ROS 2 Humble on Ubuntu 22.04
  • SIYI HM30 — Full HD video + telemetry, 30 km LOS, OFDM 2.4 GHz, AES-256 encryption, dual-antenna MIMO
  • Ground Control: QGroundControl with MAVLink telemetry overlay, live map with detected person markers
  • Data Pipeline: Detection coordinates + thermal + UWB confirmation → GCS map overlay → first responder app
  • Backup: Onboard SD card logging for post-mission analysis
  1. Pre-flight auto-check (battery, sensors, comms)
  2. Mission upload via QGroundControl (search grid / polygon)
  3. Autonomous takeoff + altitude hold (TF02-Pro + baro)
  4. Navigate waypoint grid (GNSS / VIO fallback)
  5. Continuous RGB inference (YOLOv8n @ 15 FPS)
  6. Thermal overlay scan (MLX90640 heat signatures)
  7. UWB radar life-sign confirmation (breathing, micro-motion)
  8. Multi-sensor fusion (RGB ∩ Thermal ∩ UWB → confirmed survivor)
  9. Geo-tag survivor (lat/lon/alt + timestamp + confidence)
  10. Risk assessment (triage: fire, flood, structural collapse near survivor)
  11. Priority ranking (critical > high > medium > low)
  12. Encrypted telemetry burst to GCS (SIYI HM30)
  13. Auto-RTL or proceed to next waypoint

  • Full edge-first AI — zero cloud dependency
  • Geo-tagged intelligence with risk-based prioritization
  • Hazard-aware path planning
  • ROS 2 modular architecture — field-upgradeable
